# Community code of conduct

We want Usage Desk to be a useful, welcoming project for people with different backgrounds
and levels of experience.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and make room for newcomers.
- Respect privacy, consent, and people's time.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate, or publish others' private information.
- Avoid spam, unsolicited promotion, and sexual or violent content directed at participants.

These expectations apply in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.
Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, restrict participation,
or block repeat offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and consider context.

To report an incident privately, contact the maintainer through [nikhilkr.in](https://nikhilkr.in)
and request a private conduct-reporting channel without including sensitive details initially.
Reports are handled with discretion; anonymity cannot be guaranteed if platform intervention is needed.
