Privacy

The native Usage Desk app has no cloud account, analytics, or credential-upload service.

What the app reads

The app reads account identity, plan, quota windows, and earned resets returned by your installed Codex CLI. It also reads Chrome’s Local State profile labels and emails for the optional browser picker. It never extracts browser cookies, passwords, history, or access-token databases.

For Claude profiles, Usage Desk asks the selected Claude CLI for account identity and subscription limits. Claude handles its own sign-in and network requests. Usage Desk does not read Claude credential files or browser cookies, send model prompts, change Claude settings, or scan transcripts.

Where sign-ins live

Your current CLI account stays in its existing Codex home. Added accounts get separate folders under ~/Library/Application Support/ResetDesk/accounts/. Codex manages their credentials locally. Those credentials may be sensitive files; Usage Desk does not promise that they are held in Keychain.

Removing an account stops tracking it but retains its local authentication directory. That avoids logging you out unexpectedly.

Appearance effects

The native theme transition briefly holds a picture of its own panel in memory. It is discarded after the transition or interruption and is never saved, logged, or uploaded. Reduce Motion disables the effect.

What goes over the network

Codex communicates with OpenAI for browser sign-in, credential refresh, and usage reads. OpenAI’s policies apply to Codex; Anthropic’s policies apply to Claude. Usage Desk has no intermediary server, doesn’t run model turns, and doesn’t redeem resets.

This website

The production website is configured for Vercel Web Analytics to measure aggregate visits and referrals without tracking cookies. It does not receive account data from the native app. Query strings and URL fragments are removed from page URLs before sending. Analytics is disabled on localhost and preview domains, and when your browser signals Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Ad blockers or disabled JavaScript may prevent measurement. There are no advertising scripts or external fonts. If you change its appearance, that preference is stored in your browser’s localStorage. The interactive app preview uses fixed sample accounts, as labelled. Display-name edits and preview preferences stay in memory and reset on reload; they are never sent to analytics. The preview has no access to the native app, credentials, or real accounts. The website never signs you in.

Optional custom events count successful install-command copies (hero or footer) and GitHub link clicks. These events remain off until the hosting plan supports them. They never include clipboard contents, emails, account IDs, quota values, or credentials. Copies and clicks are interest signals, not confirmed installations. The installer and native app do not report successful installs or active users.

See Vercel’s analytics privacy details. Analytics begins only after hosting activation and deployment.

A hosting provider may keep ordinary access logs under its own policy. Published GitHub downloads are subject to GitHub’s policies.

Contributing safely

Do not include real account emails, tokens, auth files, or sign-in URLs in public screenshots or reports. Use the project’s isolated demo mode for screenshots. Security-reporting instructions are in SECURITY.md in the source.
